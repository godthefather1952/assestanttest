## What the tests cover

- Provider layer: failure-class cooldowns and exponential backoff, `Retry-After`, recovery reset, stale-cache fallback with original timestamp, pacing, de-duplication and the readable health text.
- Price/liquidity separation: price stop fires with unknown liquidity and records "no fill or executable quote is assumed"; unknown liquidity leaves the position fresh; reported zero exits; unsupported market, different pool and old quotes mark STALE without exiting.
- Pool evidence: success plus failures for unknown program, wrong DEX, missing pool, mint mismatch, vault with another mint, non-token vault, duplicate vault; UNKNOWN on provider failure; UNSUPPORTED for bonding curves, Pump.fun-owned accounts and DAMM v2; Ready needs a verified pool.
- Concentration: raw versus adjusted, adjusted never discounts unverified accounts, a verified vault explaining >90% raw concentration passes while an unverified pool does not.
- Quotes: exact amount and no `taker`/credentials, unreported fields stay null, mismatched response fails closed, no-route versus provider failure, quote aging to STALE, per-size and per-position requests, definitive no-route blocks approval.
- Evidence UI: no "Verified Safety", "100/100" or coverage percentage; each row shows status, source and check time.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Approval and sell routes.** A definitive "no route" blocks approval; an unreachable quote provider only shows NOT VERIFIED. Making a fresh route mandatory is a one-line change but would block all approvals whenever the keyless quote API rate-limits.
2. **Backend.** Not built. See `provider-research-2026-10-23.md`; the recommended Cloudflare Worker + Helius free + Jupiter free-key setup costs $0 but needs account creation.
3. **Exit-time quote.** Exit signals are marked at the observed price. Taking a quote at the moment of a signal is not implemented.
