LOCAL MODEL

SD‑Turbo · 512 × 512

The first time, your browser downloads about 2.3 GB of open model files and caches them locally. After the model is loaded, generation happens on your GPU.

WHAT “PRIVATE” MEANS HERE

        

          

            No identity required.
            No login, account, API token, email address, or payment information is requested.
          

          

            Prompts stay in the tab.
            Prompts are passed to the local model in browser memory and are not saved to localStorage.
          

          

            Images stay local.
            Generated images exist as local browser blobs until you save them or clear/close the session.
          

          

            No tracking stack.
            This project contains no analytics SDK, ad tracker, telemetry endpoint, or application database.
